Thaumaturgists are those who seek and use knowledge of the arcane.  Their magic is a not a matter of faith (like the [Theurgist](PART%202%20-%20Player_Characters/Classes/Cleric.md)), but rather that of study and formulae.  Thaumaturgists are the worst of all the classes at fighting; hours spent studying massive tomes of magic do not lead a character to become strong or adept with weapons.  They are the least hardy, having the least Hit Points and worst Saving Throws.  However, their long hours of study of the arcane arts grant them awesome magical power beyond the ken of most mortals.
### Thaumaturgist Abilities
#### Detect Magic
Being sensitive to the world of magic, a Thaumaturgist may determine if a person, place or thing is magical. Doing so requires [[Concentration]] and a full minute; so they cannot tell if something is magical simply by being in its presence and people tend to notice when a spellcaster is staring at them.  After 1 minute of concentration, the Thaumaturgist may make a Class Skill (WIS) check against a target number set by the GM based on the obviousness of any magic present.  On a success, the character learns whether or not magic is present and its general type.  This can detect the presence of Theurgy as well as Thaumaturgy.

## Thaumaturgy
Thaumaturgy is the study of arcane (as opposed to divine) magic.

### Spellcasting Focus
As part of their apprenticeship, Thaumaturgists create a device to aid in casting spells. This focus may take most any form (a staff, wand, amulet or similar), but must be easily seen, recognized and hand-held. It also takes up 1 Inventory Slot. A focus may be replaced by constructing a new one which takes 1 week in a Safe Haven and 100sp worth of materials.

For every level the Thaumaturgist has, they may carve a sigil into their focus. Each sigil represents a specific spell the Thaumaturgist has learned (i.e. recorded in their Spellbook). Whenever they cast one of these spells, while holding their Magical Focus, the player rolls d8s for their Invocation Dice instead of d6s. It takes 1 Day times the spell's Tier and 50sp per Day to engrave a new sigil into a focus.

Having a Magical Focus in hand does not count against needing a free hand for spell casting.

### Invocation Dice
As spellcasters, Thaumaturgists have the number of invocation dice indicated on the table below, plus a number of additional dice equal to their Wisdom modifier. The Thaumaturgist may choose how many dice to roll each time they attempt to cast a spell.

### Spellcasting
Thaumaturgists record their spells in their spellbooks as arcane formulae. Before a spell can be cast, however, it must be prepared. The table below shows the number of spells of each level they may prepare after a full night's rest. This takes 10 uninterrupted minutes times the Rank of the spell, during which the character may take no other actions.

### Exhausting Magic
At a cost of 1 level of [[Exhaustion]], a Thaumaturgist may choose to pass an [Invocation Roll](#Invocation%20Dice) that just failed. This occurs after a normal Invocation Roll, and any expended Invocation Dice are still expended and any Mishaps still occur.

### Arcane Tradition
A Thaumaturgist must select an Arcane Tradition (Physical, Energy, Thought, Illusion, etc). This will determine which spells they have access to. See the rules on [[Magic]] for details.

## Arcane Traditions
There are twelve arcane traditions grouped into 2 general categories: Order and Entropy.  A spellcaster will typically start with a spell list made of of spells from one tradition.  However, it is possible to learn other traditions through dedicated study and practice.
### Traditions of Order
#### Chronomancy
Magic that focuses on the manipulation of time (precognition, postcognition, quickening or slowing the passage of time, etc).
#### Auramancy
Magic for the manipulation of all forms of energy (such as fire, lightning, cold, etc).
#### Materiomancy
Magic concerning itself with the physical world (stone, water, air, etc).
#### Choromancy
Magic focused on the manipulation of space/distance.
#### Cogimancy
Magic of thought and imagination.
#### Pneumamancy
The manipulation of spirits.
### Traditions of Entropy
#### Olethromancy
Destructive magic.
#### Sipsimancy
Magic of decay.
#### Stasimancy
Magic of stagnation.
#### Lacunamancy
Magic of the void.
#### Paramnemancy
Illusion
#### Necromancy
Death
