Priests are those who have devoted themselves to the service of a deity, pantheon or other supernatural patron. Most Priests spend their time in mundane forms of service such as preaching and ministering in a temple; but there are those adventurous Priests who are called to go abroad from the temple and serve their patron in a more direct way, smiting its enemies and otherwise aiding in the battle against its foes. Player character Priests are assumed to be of the adventurous variety.

## Starting Equipment
- 2d6sp
- Adventurers' Pack
- Priest's Pack

## Skills
**Example Class Tasks:** lore (religion), holy rituals and rites, persuasion and oratory.

**Detect the Supernatural:** Priests have an uncanny sense for the presence of the supernatural.  They may make a Class Skill (WIS) check to detect the presence of ghosts, demons, angels and other supernatural beings within 30 feet, even if they are invisible.  They can even detect the traces such beings leave on their surroundings after they have left (with appropriate penalties for the passage of time), and potentially can track such beings in this manner.  This sense is not refined enough, however, to give the exact position of the entity, only the sense that it is present.

## Blessings
Priests may know a variety of blessings which provide minor and temporary beneficial boosts to themselves or their allies.  No roll is required for the invocation of a blessing.  The number of known blessings is determined by the priest's level, as shown in the Priest Progression Table.  Known blessings are determined randomly.  See the chapter on [[Blessings and Miracles]].

## Miracles
Priests are granted the ability to perform miracles by their patron.  As a character advances, their patron may take note of their deeds and grant them access to new miracles.  At first level, the priest will be able to perform one randomly selected lesser miracle.  Each time the character gains a level, the player should roll for the chance to acquire the ability to perform a new miracle.  See the chapter on [[Blessings and Miracles]].****

## Patron Disfavor
Priests must follow the tenets of their religious order. Failure to do so may result in loss of class abilities until a proper penance (as determined by their superiors in the order, or the patron itself) is performed.  The required tenets vary by religion, but one thing they all have in common is requiring at least an hour of prayer/ritual each day.

## Holy Symbol
A Priest carries a Holy Symbol that's been specially consecrated by the religious order to which they belong.  This symbol is required in the invocation of both Blessings and Miracles.

## Domains of Power
Which blessing and miracles a priest may learn depends on which domains their patron has power over.  Additionally, this determines what is consider "holy" and "unholy" to the priest.  See the chapter on [[Blessings and Miracles]].
