### Autumn Folk
**Autumn Folk:** The age of the elves is long since past. A Elf’s Base and Max [[PART 1 - Introduction/Fortune|Fortune Points]] are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune).
### Burrowing
**Burrowing:** You are capable of burrowing through loose dirt and sand.  You move at 1/2 your normal ground move when burrowing.
### Camouflage
**Camouflage:** You are capable of blending in to your surroundings and gain [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on Stealth checks.  However, it takes 5 minutes to adapt to the new colors of new surroundings.
### Climbing Claws
**Climbing Claws:** You have retractable claws suitable for climbing.  This grants you [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on Climbing checks involving surfaces softer than stone.  In melee combat, your claws may be used to do 1d4 slashing damage.
### Colorblind (warm)
**Colorblind (warm):** Colors in the warm end of the spectrum (yellow through red) appear as shades of grey to you.
### Colorblind (cool)
**Colorblind (cool):** Colors in the cool end of the spectrum (green through purple) appear as shades of grey to you.
### Colorblind (full)
**Colorblind (full):** All colors appear as shades of grey to you.
### Desert Adaptation
**Desert Adaptation:** You only require 1/3 the amount of water that a human does.  Additionally, you do not suffer from extremely hot environments (up to 160f / 71c).
### Dust of Ages
**Dust of Ages:** The age of the dwarf is long since past. A Dwarf’s Base and Max [[PART 1 - Introduction/Fortune|Fortune Points]] are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune). ^6c7005
### Fae Blooded
**Fae Blooded:** As a fae being, your inherently magical nature grants you [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on all Saving Throws vs magic.
### Far-Sighted
**Far-Sighted:** You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on perception checks to recognize detail on any object within 5 feet.
### Fallen Empire
**Fallen Empire:** The age of the reptile ended long ago. A Lizard-folk’s Base and Max [[PART 1 - Introduction/Fortune|Fortune Points]] are reduced by 1 (to a minimum 0 Base Fortune and 1 Max Fortune).
### Fortitude of Iron
**Fortitude of Iron:** You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on Saving Throws vs Poison and Disease.
### Giant Strength
**Giant Strength:** You increase your Strength by +4 for determining the size of your Readied and Stowed gear lists.
### Hardy
**Hardy:** You can withstand massive punishment and recover quickly.  You gain +1 HP per level and roll Hit Dice with [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]].
### Hob Spirit
**Hob Spirit:** Hobs raise the spirits of even the most downtrodden and are valued by all their companions. So long as they can see and hear him, all of a Hob's companions within 30 feet gain [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on Saving Throws to resist Fear and despair and NPC allies gain Advantage on Morale checks.
### Hooves
**Hooves:** Your feet are actually hooves.  While well-suited to going without footwear in all types of terrain, they are ill-suited to slippery surfaces.  You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] on Dexterity Checks to balance on slippery surfaces.
### Infravision
**Infravision:** While in dim or dark conditions, you can see heat sources.  Heat sources appear in shades of yellow to red only, while cool objects appear in shades of light grey to black (for the coldest objects).  Objects close to the ambient temperature are effectively invisible to you.
### Intrepid
**Intrepid:** Your Base and Max [[PART 1 - Introduction/Fortune|Fortune Points]] are each increased by 1.
### Iron Allergy
**Iron Allergy:** Iron and steel weapons do +1 damage to you.
### Keen Sense of Smell
**Keen Sense of Smell:** You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on any perception or survival check involving your sense of smell.
### Keen Senses
**Keen Senses:** You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on Perception checks.
### Leap
**Leap:** Add +4 to your STR for the purposes of calculating leaping distance (horizontal & vertical).
### Lightsleep
**Lightsleep:** You are difficult to catch napping.  You do not suffer [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] to Surprise checks while sleeping.
### Longsleep
**Longsleep:** You require more rest than most other species.  You require 12 hours of rest to gain the benefits of a Long Rest.
### Low-Light Vision
**Low-Light Vision:** You see in dim light nearly as well as full daylight; however, this vision is shades of gray only, so color cannot be discerned in these conditions.  Also, this ability does not function in full darkness.
### Lung Capacity
**Lung Capacity:** You can hold your breath for up to 20 minutes before you risk drowning.
### Massive
**Massive:** You treat two-handed melee weapons as if they were Versatile and Versatile weapons as if they were one-handed (while still gaining [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on damage rolls) and you can wield Medium weapons in your off-hand as if they were Light. However, your large hands wield actual Light weapons with [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] and any armor built for your size costs 4 times the normal amount and weights twice as much. Finally, you must eat and drink 4 times as much as a human.
### Natural Weapons (claws)
**Natural Weapons:** Your clawed fingers mean you are never truly unarmed.  You may make a claw attack in melee to do 1d6 slashing damage.
### Natural Weapons (fangs)
**Natural Weapons:** A mouth full of vicious fangs means you are never truly unarmed.  You may make a bite attack in melee to do 1d6 piercing damage.
### Natural Weapons (horns)
**Natural Weapons:** Great horns protruding from your head mean you are never truly unarmed.  You may make a headbutt attack in melee to do 1d6 bludgeoning damage.
### Relentless Endurance
**Relentless Endurance:** You do not tire easily and have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on any Saving Throw or Ability Check to resist gaining a level of Exhaustion.  
### Scales
**Scales:** You have protective scales.  This grants you a +2 AC bonus when wearing light armor or less.
### Small Stature
**Small Stature:** Being small hurts in a fight. You are a small creature and have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Disadvantage]] wielding any weapon with the Heavy property, or when wielding a Versatile weapon one-handed.  You also halve your total leaping distance (horizontal and vertical).

On the other hand, you gain [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on all Stealth checks.
### Strong Back
**Strong Back:** You increase your Strength by +2 for determining the size of your Readied and Stowed gear lists.
### Sure-Footed (mountain)
**Sure-Footed (mountain):** You are well-adapted to climbing rocky ledges and steep slopes.  You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on balance checks caused by such terrain and you do not suffer its movement penalties.
### Sure-Footed (sand)
**Sure-Footed (sand):** You are well-adapted to running across the sandy slopes of dunes.  You have [[PART 1 - Introduction/Using the Dice#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on balance checks caused by such terrain and you do not suffer its movement penalties.
### Swimming
**Swimming:** You can swim at the same speed as your walking rate.  You do not incur movement or Dexterity penalties from being in water.
### Thick Fur
**Thick Fur:** You have dense, coarse fur.  This grants you a +1 AC when wearing light armor or less.
### Thick Hide
**Thick Hide:** You have a thick leathery hide.  This grants you a +1 AC when wearing light armor or less.
### True Name
**True Name:** As a supernatural being, you have a True Name, and those with the knowledge may use it against you. By calling out your True Name, a creature gains [[PART 1 - Introduction/Introduction#Advantage and Disadvantage|Advantage]] on all actions taken against you, including attacks and Saving Throws.
### Vanish
**Vanish:** You have an uncanny ability to blend in with your surroundings.  So long as you are in a natural environment with at least minimal cover, you may become **Invisible** for up to 1 minute.  Your invisibility ends early if you move or attack.  Once you have used this ability, you cannot use it again until you have completed a long rest.
### Vulnerability (cold)
**Vulnerability (cold):** You take double damage from ==cold== attacks.
### Wide Vision
**Wide Vision:** You are capable of seeing in a 300 degree arc.
