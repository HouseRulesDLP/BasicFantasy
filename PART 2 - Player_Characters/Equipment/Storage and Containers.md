#### Barrels
The standard for storing various liquids. A barrel is generally made by having tight fitting slats tied together with a band of steel or iron. Aging barrels for alcoholic beverages will be charred by fire on the inside to help with the aging process. Barrels for beer, wine, or any other liquid will not be charred. This list gives a common range of barrel sizes used for liquids and brewing.

#### Baskets
Strips of green bark, willow branches, or reeds woven together to form a container; once the material is dried out it will hold its shape. Because there are some large gaps in these types of containers, they are not meant for holding grains, but will hold larger fruit and provide some air flow to prevent the fruits from becoming too wet or spoiling sooner.

#### Caskets
Specially-designed boxes that hold the body of a humanoid. Used for disposal of bodies after death, if the local religion uses burial as a death rite. The price listed is for a plain pinewood box. There can be added cost for fancier caskets that include engraving, fancier or exotic woods or metal sides; use [Quality](#Quality) adjustments for pricing.

#### Chests
Wooden boxes with lids; the sides are made tight to prevent anything from getting out or into the box. The price is for a basic box; consider a sea chest to be a piece of [Excellent Quality](#Quality) since it will have a lining that prevents its contents from getting wet even if immersed in water. A **hidden compartment** is a concealed space in a chest, usually no more than an inch in thickness. This can be a hidden gap along one side, a false bottom, or a secret drawer on the outside of the chest. A successful Search roll will reveal the compartment if the opening area is being searched (a false bottom will only be found by examining the inside of the chest, for example). The price listed for a hidden compartment is in addition to the cost of the chest.

A **Traveling Chest** is one designed for ease of movement. Special materials and design allows it to be lighter than normal, and wheels and a lead are added, effectively turning it into a small wagon. The price listed is in addition to the initial cost of the size of the chest.

A **Strongbox** comes equipped with a lock (diff 15 to pick, 18 to break) and is reinforced against damage.

#### Crates
Wooden box that uses thins slats nailed to corner pieces. The slats are generally one to three inches (2.5 to 7.5 cm) wide and have a space of ¾ to 1.5 inches (2 to 3.75 cm) between slats on the sides. There is generally no lid or if there is the slats will use the same spacing as the sides. These are generally used for fruits and vegetables or for small animals, like chickens or smaller.
