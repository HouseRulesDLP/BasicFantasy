## Services
There are a number of jobs that NPCs can take to help support player characters. These jobs can help the player characters understand where their hard-got gains are being spent. They can also be sources of information outside the typical tavern setting.

### Food and Shelter
While on an adventure, your character will still need food to eat and a place to sleep. Travel rations and a thin bedroll on hard cold ground may be sustaining, but they’re hardly comfortable. ### Amenity Descriptions
#### Apartment
Apartments are just the rooms for sleeping and staying in, similar to a hotel. Common amenities include having the room cleaned at least 3 times a week. In larger city settings rooms may have to be shared with 4 other PCs and/or NPCs. Poor amenities mean the rooms cleaned once a week if even then, and there is always a good chance that there will be bedbugs or other creepy crawlies in the beds. The beds must also be shared with 8 to 12 other PCs and/or NPCs.
#### Drinks
Alcoholic drinks are not necessarily consumed to get drunk in the medieval worlds as usually portrayed. Water sources were not always as clean and pure as one might wish; beverages containing alcohol were much safer to consume.
##### Beers and Ales
Brewed or fermented from grains that use yeast to make the alcohol content. These are generally not as potent as spirits and are usually produced at monasteries.
##### Cider
Fermented fruit juices, generally non-citrus juices like apple, pear, and peach and many different berries. Citruses like orange, lime, and lemon have acids that kill the yeast used to ferment the juice.
##### Mead
Honey diluted with water or apple juice, fermented with wine yeast. A potent drink popular in northern climates.
##### Small Beer
A low-alcohol beer. This lightly-fermented brew is almost a liquid snack, and keeps clean better than water. Suitable for adventuring, though it may turn ‘dank’ if left in a wineskin for too long.
##### Spirits
Fermented alcohols that are boiled again to remove the water and increase the alcohol content. The type of spirit is dependent on the grain, grape, fruit, or berry used as a base. These are generally made by a local farmer. Quality depends on the experience of the person making the spirit and the quality of the crop.
##### Wine
Fermented grape juices; the type of grapes determine the color and flavor of the wine. Monasteries produce the best wines.

#### Grain and Stabling
This is usually found at a Livery or Stable. A livery is an independent storefront that offers housing for horses, including farrier service and tack. The farrier will feed and care for the horses as part of the housing service. A stable is a barn for housing horses associated with an inn or rooming house. The owners of the horses are responsible for feeding their animals; the inn will charge to replenish the grain and hay eaten by the horses.
#### Inn Lodging
Inn lodging includes a room and a day’s meals. Higher quality stays may include a private room that is cleaned on a daily basis and good-quality meals, and not having to share the room or bed with anyone else. Common lodgings include sharing a room with another person, with common meals provided. The rooms are cleaned about 3 times a week. Poor lodgings will include lackluster meals and having to sleep in the common room with many others.

### Service Descriptions
#### Bath
Includes tub and hot water. Soaps, oils, or other ‘amenities’ are extra.
#### Bribes
Occasionally the wheels of civilization must be greased.  Prices vary based on both how highly placed the official is and what it is they are expected to do/overlook.
#### Coach/Cab
Transportation for a passenger + light cargo.  Travelling overland (outside of town) will cost extra if there are known hazards along the route (bandits, raiders, monsters, etc).  Guards to protect the passengers are a separate expense (see Mercenaries, below).
#### Crimes
Hiring a criminal to perform some illegal task.  Minor crimes might include petty theft, forgery and the like.  Capital crimes are those punishable by death or torture.
#### Messenger
A Messenger (usually associated with a Clerk) will deliver a letter or other document that can easily be transported in a saddle bag.
#### Minstrel
A minstrel’s performance will usually last an hour. During the performance the minstrel will sing several popular songs and may tell a few jokes.
#### Mourner
A person paid to attend a funeral; may also be a pall bearer if there are not enough people at the funeral (add another 1 cp in this case).
#### Road or Gate Toll
A per-person/animal toll; wagons and goods will cost extra. Usually paid upon entry into a city or town.
#### Ship's Passage
Common accommodations include a shared stateroom (for up to 4 people), light cargo and meals.  Poor accommodations require sleeping with the crew with only as many items as the passenger can personally carry.
#### Spellcasting
If a town or city has a competent spellcaster, they can be employed to cast a spell on behalf of the hiring PC.  Typical costs are 100sp times the spell's rank to the power of 2, plus any material costs.  For example, a 3rd level spell would cost 100 * 3^2 = 100 * 9 = 900sp.  
### Hirelings
Hirelings are NPCs the characters may employ to assist with menial tasks (such as maintaining camps or porting goods) as well as more specialized services the PCs may not have the skill to perform, themselves (such as masonry, smithing, or the like).  Hirelings are rated by their skill level, with a typical hireling being of the Journeyman rank.  In areas where a guild controls a trade, it is often illegal to hire apprentices without first consulting their mentor.

Expert crafters of various types (armorer, blacksmith, carpenter, mason, weaponsmith, etc.) may be employed to assist in making magical artifacts related to their craft.

These are the common craftspersons and laborers that can be found in any reasonably sized town or larger community. Villages and thorps will have similar services available, at least in a nearby community. These types of hirelings are typically employed on a daily basis (or even up to a full week). Longer-term jobs are typically not favored by these workers for fear of backlogs with other clients. Likewise, most won't be willing to travel more than a 1/2 day to a day from home without extra compensation (at least food, lodging and a couple extra silvers per week). ##### Alchemist    
These characters are generally hired for one of two reasons: to make potions, or to assist with magical research.

An alchemist can produce a potion, given the required materials and a sample or a written formula for the potion, in the same time and for the same
cost as an [Mage](PART%202%20-%20Player_Characters/Classes/Mage.md).  They may also research new potions, but at twice the cost in time and materials as an Arcanist.

Alternately, an Arcanist seeking to create certain magic items may employ an alchemist as an assistant. In this case, the alchemist counts as taking the Help action.
##### Animal Handler
(aka Groom, Ostler, Stable Hand) Feeds, grooms and otherwise takes care of animals.  Can care for up to twice their Skill Bonus [Hit Dice](0_Monster_Descriptions.md#Hit%20Dice) worth of animals at a time.
##### Animal Trainer
Characters wishing to ride hippogriffs or employ carnivorous apes as guards will need the assistance of an animal trainer. The cost above is for an average animal trainer, able to train one type of “normal” animal such as carnivorous apes; those able to train more than one sort of animal, or to train monstrous creatures such as hippogriffs, are more expensive to hire. The Game Master must decide how long it takes to train an animal; in some cases, animal training may take years, a fact the player characters may find inconvenient as well as expensive. A single animal trainer can train and manage no more than double their skill bonus in [Hit Dice](0_Monster_Descriptions.md#Hit%20Dice) worth of animals at a time, though in most cases once an animal is fully trained, if it is put into service right away the animal trainer won't be needed to handle it any longer.
##### Advocate
Educated in matters of local/kingdom law and procedure, advocates may represent their clients before the courts and draw up legal contracts.
##### Apothecary
An apothecary prepares drugs and medicines.  They can create up to 4 [Healer's Kits](#Healer's%20Kit) worth of medicine per month.
##### Armorer
A single armorer can produce 1,000gp worth of Medium or Heavy Armor per month (+/- 50% for each rank above or below [Journeyman](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks)).  For every 3 assistants (30sp/month/assistant), this output may be doubled.  One armorer may benefit from no more than 6 assistants.

Characters hiring mercenaries, or having armed and armored followers to take care of, will need the services of an armorer. In general, for every 50 soldiers employed, one armorer is required to care for their gear. Each assistant increases this number by 10.

[Expert](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks) and higher ranked armorers may be employed to assist in making magic armor.  In this case, the armorer is considered to be taking the Help action.
##### Barber
Barbers provide grooming services along with minor surgeries, dentistry and other medical procedures.
##### Blacksmith
Essential for the maintenance of almost any community and any resident soldiery; for every blacksmith retained the needs of up to one hundred and twenty men or horses can be met. If sufficient time is available, a blacksmith can produce simple weaponry worth no more than 10sp in value.
##### Bowyer
(aka Fletcher) A skilled artisan capable of producing bows (or other ranged weapons) along with their needed ammunition.  There must be at least one bowyer available for every 50 archers/crossbowmen retained. The bowyer must be provided with a workroom and tools at an additional cost.  A single bowyer can make up to 400sp worth of ranged weapons per month. For every 3 assistants (15sp/month/assistant), the output may be doubled.  One bowyer may benefit from no more than 6 assistants.

[Expert](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks) and higher ranked bowyers may be employed to assist in making magical ranged weapons.  In this case, the bowyer is considered to be taking the Help action.
##### Carpenter
Skilled in the working of wood, a carpenter might be retained to construct anything from a table to a palisade. Their expertise is also invaluable for the manufacturing of shields and similar items.
##### Cartographer
(aka mapmaker) Cartographers are skilled in the reading and making of maps.  They will not enter dungeons or other highly dangerous areas and, instead, redraw maps based on their patron's notes, drawings and descriptions.
##### Cook
Familiar with the preparation of various types of food, a good cook sometimes also knows a little of herb lore.  A single cook can prepare meals for up to 10 [Medium](0_Monster_Descriptions.md#Size) sized people.
##### Crafter
This is a catch-all category for any crafts-person not explicitly called out in this section.  Such people would include wainwrights (wagon makers), coopers (barrel-makers), cobblers (shoe makers), tailors and so forth.
##### Engineer
Anyone wishing to build a fortress, ship, or any other large construction will need an engineer.  The price on the table above is simply for retaining the engineer's services.  An additional fee equal to 10% of the projects total cost is expected upon completion of the project.  This category also includes architects.
##### Guide
A city guide is someone that knows all the hot- and low-spots of the city and most of the dealings that are going on between different criminal organizations.
A wilderness guide knows the paths and trails off the main roads and will know where most common points-of-interest and dangers are located.
##### Herbalist
Knowledgeable of local plants a herbalist must be supplied with a garden and tools.  Additionally, they will enter the wilderness to procure those plants that only grow in the wild.  A single herbalist can craft up to 4 [Herbalist Kits](#Herbalist%20Kit) or 2 [Healer's Kits](#Healer's%20Kit) per month.
##### Interpreter
Knowledgeable in additional languages equal to half their Skill Bonus, interpreters serve as translators and often work with clerks and scribes in translating written works.
##### Jeweler
Able to speedily and accurately appraise the value of most gems, jewelry and other precious objects, a jeweler is also capable of repairing, enhancing or newly creating ornamented items and jewelry. Simple tasks, such as setting a stone in the hilt of a sword or the forging of a plain ring, might take only a few days to a week, whilst more complex undertakings, such as producing a gem studded bracelet, could take up to a month; of course, truly opulent and intricate items might take a year or more.

Often a jeweler is also skilled at cutting gems; just as in their former capacity they may increase the value of an item through their craft, as a gem-cutter they may increase the value of poorly cut stones, usually those worth less than five thousand silver pieces. However, the level of proficiency an individual possesses with regard to each skill varies and must be determined by the game master.
##### Laborer
Essentially "unskilled", laborer's are suitable for only the most menial sorts of work; this category includes bearers and porters. Each laborer can carry 10 [Stowed](1_Dungeon_Adventures.md#Stowed%20Items) items.  Two laborers utilizing a pole, liter or similar conveyance increases their capacity by 50% (to 30 stowed items between the pair).
##### Leather Worker
Capable of producing a wide range of leather goods, such as packs, belts or riding gear; a leatherer is indispensable for the making of scabbards, sheathes, shields and the other leather components of arms and armor.  A leatherworker can craft up to 200sp worth of Light Armor in a month.  For every 3 assistants (1cp/day/assistant), the output may be doubled.  One leatherworker may benefit from no more than 6 assistants.

[Expert](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks) and higher ranked leather workers may be employed to assist in making magical items made from leather or hide.  In this case, the leather worker is considered to be taking the Help action.
##### Limner
Employed to illustrate books, paint signs and design heraldry.
##### Linkboy
(aka Torchbearer or Lanternbearer) Usually hired to bear a lantern or torch, a linkboy is typically a youth, but older individuals are not unknown.  Will charge double the normal rate for dangerous duty (such as entering a dungeon).
##### Mason
Expert in the working of stone or plaster, masons are essential for the construction of many significant buildings and fortifications as the prepare the construction materials needed by the engineers and laborers.
##### Miner
Indispensable for the overseeing of any mining operations, underground construction, trench-work and ditch-work.
##### Navigator
A navigator is someone who understands how to read charts and maps, and can navigate based on instruments and the position of the stars.  Ship and overland navigators are separate specialties.
##### Physic
A learned medical practitioner formally trained in the arts of medicine and surgery.
##### Sage
Sages are experts in ancient and obscure knowledge. Many sages have particular interests in very limited or focused areas (for example, “Elven migrations of the 2nd age”), but even these will know or have access to a lot of general facts. The listed cost is the minimum required to maintain a sage with a library, collections, etc. A sage will typically have 1 area of specialized knowledge for every 2 points of their Skill Bonus (rounded down) and an equal number of minor interests (half normal skill bonus for such knowledge checks).

Alternatively a sage may be consulted on a per-question basis, typically at a cost of 10sp per question per day of research.
##### Servant
Typically serving as valets, butlers, maids, messengers or simple lackeys, servants are expected to look to the needs of their employer.
##### Scribe
Practiced in the art of writing, a typical scribe is expected to keep records, write letters and copy documents. Others may possess additional skills, such as cartography, counterfeiting, cryptography, illuminating or the ability to write, read or otherwise comprehend more than one language. Such accomplished individuals might command ten times the standard wage.
##### Spy/Assassin
A spy is hired to gather information about a person or group, while an [Assassin](3_Mercenaries.md#Assassin) will eliminate said person or group. They may be an outsider who tries to infiltrate or may be a traitor already connected with the target person or group.
##### Steward
Responsible for the administration of a stronghold in the absence or inability of the owning noble, a steward holds a position of great prestige and trust. Usually, such an individual is promoted from a soldier of the rank of captain or higher, and will not afterwards take kindly to being asked to reassume that role, even temporarily. Whilst serving within the stronghold, a typical is capable of leading 40 soldiers and 2 lieutenants per point of [Skill Bonus](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks), as well as the necessary number of sergeants. Given that a fortress is well provisioned, garrisoned and supported at the time at which he is appointed, a steward will see to it that such remains the case. Of course, if a player character expects her dependents to vigorously resist any enemy action in her absence or incapacity, she must ensure that the loyalty of such soldiers is strongly maintained.
##### Tailor
Accomplished in the repair and making of clothes or other cloth items, such as surcoats, capes or hats; the services of a tailor are also required for the production of various types of textiles.
##### Teamster
Trained in driving carts/wagons and will help load/unload their vehicle.
##### Weaponsmith
Required for the production of complex weaponry and the maintenance of the arms of any soldiery; there must be at least one weaponsmith available for every 50 soldiers retained. The weaponsmith must be provided with a workroom and forge at an additional cost, but sufficient apprentices and assistants are assumed in his monthly wage.  A single weaponsmith can make up to 400sp worth of weapons per month. For every 3 assistants (30sp/month/assistant), the output may be doubled.  One weapon smith may benefit from no more than 6 assistants.

[Expert](#Hireling%20Skill%20Ranks) and higher ranked armorers may be employed to weaponsmith in making magic weapons.  In this case, the weaponsmith is considered to be taking the Help action.
