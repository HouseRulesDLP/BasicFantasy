#### Description
Camels are known for their ability to travel long distances without food or water.  The statistics presented here are for the dromedary, or one-humped camel, which thrives in warm deserts.  A dromedary stands about 7 feet tall at the shoulder, with its hump rising 1 foot higher.  The two-humped, or Bactrian, camel is suited to cooler, rocky areas.  It is stockier, slower (speed given in brackets), and tougher than the dromedary.  A camel can carry up to 400 pounds (80 item slots) unencumbered.

Camels can be trained for war.  Such camels use the morale value given in parenthesis.

#### Description
**Pet dogs** include most medium and large breeds.  After biting an opponent, a dog can hold on, doing 1d4 damage automatically every round, until killed or until the victim spends an attack breaking free (which requires an opposed Strength roll).

**Hunting/Riding dogs** are a large breed (mastiffs or wolfhounds, for example), used primarily by Halflings for transport (the same stats can also be used for sled dogs).  They can maintain a hold in the same way that normal dogs do.  A riding dog can carry up to 150 pounds (30 item slots) without penalty.

**Guard/War dogs** are also large breed dogs, but these have been trained for fighting.  Like all dogs, they have keen  senses of smell and hearing, making them adept at detecting intruders. Most war  dogs are not  usually vicious,  and  will  rarely  attack without cause. The status of war dogs varies greatly; some are loyal and beloved pets, others are watch dogs, and some are trained for battle. 

#### Special Abilities
**Keen senses:** Dogs have *Advantage* on perception checks involving smell or hearing.

#### Description
**Elephants** are massive herbivores of tropical lands, that are sometimes used as mounts or beasts of burden. An elephant can carry up to 10,000 pounds (2,000 item slots) unencumbered.  An elephant has no treasure as such, but the tusks of an elephant are worth 1d8 x 100 sp.  An elephant trained for war uses the morale value shown in parenthesis.

**Mammoths** are an ancestor of the elephant that was common during the Pleistocene era. Mammoths are covered with thicker, woolier hair than elephants and they are considerably larger.  Mammoths are much more aggressive than elephants and will attack with less provocation. Because they are heavier, a mammoths tusks are worth 50% more than an elephant's. Mammoths are rare when encountered outside of a lost-world style campaign. and will only be found in subarctic plains. A mammoth can carry 15,000 pounds (3,000 item slots) unencumbered, but they are very rarely suitable for domestication.

#### Description
**Donkeys** are long-eared, horse-like creatures.  They are surefooted and sturdy, and can be taken into dungeons or caverns.  The statistics presented here could also describe burros and mules.  A donkey can carry up to 150 pounds (30 item slots) unencumbered.

**Draft Horses** include large breeds of working horses such as Clydesdales.  These animals are usually ready for heavy work by age three.  A draft horse can carry up to 350 pounds (70 item slots) unencumbered.

**Riding Horses** include smaller breeds of working horses such as quarter horses and Arabians as well as wild horses.  These animals are usually ready for useful work by age two.  Riding horses cannot fight while a rider is mounted.  A riding horse can carry up to 250 pounds (50 item slots) unencumbered.

**War Horses** are trained and bred for strength and aggression. They usually are not ready for warfare before age three.  A light warhorse can carry up to 300 pounds (60 item slots) unencumbered.  A medium warhorse can carry up to 350 pounds (70 item slots) unencumbered.  A heavy warhorse can carry up to 400 pounds (80 item slots) unencumbered.

A **Pony** is a small horse, under 5 feet tall at the shoulder.  Ponies are otherwise similar to riding horses and cannot fight while carrying a rider.  Ponies can be trained for war, and the morale in parentheses above is for a war pony; this does not allow them to fight while carrying a rider.  A pony can carry up to 275 pounds (55 item slots) unencumbered.

#### Description
**Bonobos** and **Chimpanzees** are closely related social apes.  While short, both can weight as much as an adult human and are far stronger.  While generally herbivorous, they will eat fresh meat when it's available, even hunting small animals.  Bonobos are rarer and much less aggressive than chimpanzees.

**Carnivorous Aps** resemble gorillas but are far more aggressive; though they are actually omnivores, they prefer meat, and they kill and eat anything they can catch. An adult male carnivorous ape is 5-1/2 to 6 feet tall and weighs 300 to 400 pounds.  They can both bite and claw at a target in the same round.

**Gigantopithecus**  are   prehistoric   gorillas,   huge   and powerful.  It is a vegetarian like their lesser kin, but can be as vicious as the carnivorous varieties if provoked.

**Girallons** are the savage, four-armed, magical cousins of the gorilla.   An adult girallon is about 8 feet tall, broad-chested, and covered in thick fur.   It weighs about 800 pounds.  A solitary girallon usually conceals itself, attacking with  surprise.   When a girallon spots or smells prey, it charges.  A girallon picks up prey that is small enough to carry and withdraws, often vanishing into the trees before the victim’s companions can retaliate.

**Gorillas** are generally herbivores; it is aggressive mainly in defense of its family group.   Otherwise it tends to avoid adventurers.

**Orangutans** are usually heavier than humans (averaging 225 to 250 pounds or so) and stronger.  Orangutans are shy creatures and would generally prefer to be left alone, but they are physically powerful and may be very dangerous if cornered or provoked.

#### Special Abilities
**Rend:** Any ape that hits with 2 fists or claws automatically grapples its target.  If that target does not escape, the ape will rend the target the following round, automatically hitting.

**Excellent Climbers:** Apes can use their full ground movement while climbing and have advantage on DEX checks related to climbing.

#### Description
Bears attack by rending opponents with their claws, dragging them in and biting them. A successful hit with both paws indicates a hug attack for additional damage (as given for each specific bear type).  All bears are very tough to kill, and are able to move and attack for one round after losing all hit points.

The **black bear** is a forest-dwelling omnivore that usually is not dangerous unless an interloper threatens its cubs or food supply.  They may actually be pure black, blond, or cinnamon in color.  They are rarely more than 5 feet long.

The monstrous **cave bear** is even larger than the grizzly bear.  They are ferocious killers, attacking almost anything of equal or smaller size.

**Grizzly bears** are massive carnivores that weigh more than 1,800 pounds and stand nearly 9 feet tall when they rear up on their hind legs. They are bad-tempered and territorial.

**Polar bears** are long, lean carnivores, slightly taller than grizzly bears, and just as hostile.

#### Description

**Jaguar:** These great cats are about 8 to 9 feet long (from nose to tail-tip) and weigh about 165 pounds.  Unlike other great cats, they enjoy swimming and often hunt near rivers or lakes.  Jaguars kill with their powerful bite, preferring to deliver a fatal wound to the skull of their prey.

**Lion:** The statistics presented here describe a male lion, which is 5 to 8 feet long and weighs 330 to 550 pounds.  Females are slightly smaller but use the same statistics.

**Spotted Lion:** Spotted lions are large, fierce, dappled versions of the lion. They are very rare outside of lost world scenarios. 

**Tiger:** These great cats stand more than 3 feet tall at the shoulder and are about 9 feet long. They weigh from 400 to 600 pounds.

**Smilodon:** The sabre-tooth cat, or smilodon, is a prehistoric great cat with very large canine teeth.  They behave much as do mountain lions or jaguars. They are very rare outside of lost world scenarios. 

#### Special Abilities
**Rake:** If a large cat hits a single target with both claw attacks, that target becomes grappled.  Until the target escapes, the cat's attacks will be 1 Bite and 1 Rake each round, with the rake auto-hitting.

**Low-Light Vision:** Great cats have low-light vision out to 120'.

#### Description

**Cheetah:** A Cheetah is one of the fastest land animals - a large (about 100 pounds) cat capable of reaching up to 75 miles per hour when running.  It hunts alone or in small groups (usually composed of siblings).  It will rarely attack humans unless compelled to do so, but a female will ferociously defend her young.

**Jaguar:** These great cats are about 8 to 9 feet long (from nose to tail-tip) and weigh about 165 pounds.  Unlike other great cats, they enjoy swimming and often hunt near rivers or lakes.  Jaguars kill with their powerful bite, preferring to deliver a fatal wound to the skull of their prey.

**Leopard:** Leopards are 4 feet long and weigh 120 pounds. The statistics presented here can also be used for panthers.

**Lion:** The statistics presented here describe a male lion, which is 5 to 8 feet long and weighs 330 to 550 pounds.  Females are slightly smaller but use the same statistics.

**Mountain Lion:** (aka Cougar) These great cats are about 7 feet long (from nose to tail-tip) and weigh about 140 pounds.  They see well in darkness and may be found hunting day or night.

**Spotted Lion:** Spotted lions are large, fierce, dappled versions of the lion. They are very rare outside of lost world scenarios. 

**Lynx, Giant:** This is a giant version of the common lynx, typically only found in Faerie or near a Faerie gate.  They have thick, gray fur that is spotted, striped, or plain, and a short tail. The giant lynx is 4 to 6 feet long and weighs around 100 pounds.

**Tiger:** These great cats stand more than 3 feet tall at the shoulder and are about 9 feet long. They weigh from 400 to 600 pounds.

**Smilodon:** The sabre-tooth cat, or smilodon, is a prehistoric great cat with very large canine teeth.  They behave much as do mountain lions or jaguars. They are very rare outside of lost world scenarios. 

#### Special Abilities
**Rake:** If a large cat hits a single target with both claw attacks, that target becomes grappled.  Until the target escapes, the cat's attacks will be 1 Bite and 1 Rake each round, with the rake auto-hitting.

**Low-Light Vision:** Hunting cats have low-light vision out to 120'.

#### Description
A typical giant eagle stands about 10 feet tall, has a wingspan of up to 20 feet, and resembles its smaller cousins in nearly every way except size. It weighs about 500 pounds. Many giant eagles are intelligent creatures and speak Common.

A giant eagle typically attacks from a great height, diving earthward at tremendous speed. When it cannot dive, it uses its powerful talons and slashing beak to strike at its target’s head and eyes.

A solitary giant eagle is typically hunting or patrolling in the vicinity of its nest and generally ignores creatures that do not appear threatening. A mated pair attacks in concert, making repeated diving attacks to drive away intruders, and fights to the death to defend their nest or hatchlings.

#### Description
A **Small Monkey** weighs about 30-40 pounds.  They are  usually  shy,  but if  one  or  more  of  them becomes comfortable around humans they can be quite a nuisance. This smaller type of monkey is generally inconsequential to adventurers.

A **Medium Monkey** (such as a baboon) weighs 60-90 pounds.   It lives in packs and often hunts other small game.  It will face down predators on occasion. Normally, they will not present a threat to adventurers, but if cornered or threatened they will become hostile.

A **Giant Monkey** weights around 180 pounds and stands as tall as an adult human.  More aggressive than their smaller cousins, they will potentially attack groups of adventurers who appear weakened or are fewer in number than the money pack.

#### Special Abilities
**Excellent Climbers:** Monkeys of all varieties can use their full ground movement while climbing and have advantage on DEX checks related to climbing.

#### Description
**Owls** hunt rodents, mall lizards, and insects, attacking humans only when frightened (or magically commanded). They have 120' Low-light Vision  and *Advantage* on hearing perception checks. They fly in near total silence, gaining *Advantage* on stealth checks.  Their eye sight is worse than that of humans, suffering Disadvantage on sight perception checks in full daylight.

A typical **giant owl** stands about 9 feet tall, has a wingspan of up to 20 feet, and resembles its smaller cousins in nearly every way.  A giant owl attacks by gliding silently just a few feet above its prey and plunging to strike when directly overhead.  It has the same senses and stealth abilities as its smaller cousins.

#### Description
**Wolves** are pack hunters known for their persistence and cunning.  They prefer to surround and flank a foe when they can.

**Dire wolves** are efficient pack hunters that will kill anything they can catch.  Dire wolves are generally mottled gray or black, about 9 feet long and weighing some 800 pounds.
