# The Magical Arts
## Spellbooks
[Arcanists](Arcanist.md) and other arcane spellcasters record their spells and cantrips in "spellbooks". These do not have to be literal books, but rather may be collections of scrolls, arcane formula carved into wood or most any other device the player and GM can agree on. The primary limitation is that the spellbook be separate from the character (arcane tattoos are not a spellbook). The language in which spells are written are typically a magical cipher learned from the caster's teacher and modified in their own way. As such, deciphering someone else's spellbook can be a long and arduous process.

A [typical spellbook](General%20Equipment.md#Spellbook) (aka "reference" spellbook) costs 200sp and can hold up to 256 pages worth of spells.  A travel spellbook is considerably more compact, costs 100sp and only contains 64 pages worth of spells.
