# Tier 1 Spells
## Abjuration
- **Arcane Traditions:** Necromancy, Thaumaturgy
- **Divine Domains:** Death, Life, Light
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Duration:** 1 week per caster level
- **Range:** 10'
- **Area:** 1 spirit
- **Save:** doom
- **Components:** gestures & incantations

The spellcaster performs a rite of banishing, and attempts to send a single incorporeal spirit back to its home realm. If the target fails its saving throw it is immediately returned to its home realm, from which it cannot return for the spell's Duration. This spell only affects spirits not of the mortal world who have found their way here from another dimension.

*Upcasting*
Casting this spell at a higher tier increases both the range and number of spirits affected.  If cast at tier 4+, this spell may affect corporeal creatures from other dimensions (see table below).

| Tier | Range | Area                    | Duration                 |
| :--: | :---: | ----------------------- | ------------------------ |
|  2   |  20'  | 2 spirits               | 1 fortnight/caster level |
|  3   |  30'  | 3 spirits               | 1 month/caster level     |
|  4   |  60'  | 4 spirits or 1 creature | 1 season/caster level    |
|  +1  | +30'  | +1 spirit/creature      | 1 year/caster level      |
## Alarm
- **Arcane Traditions:** Runic
- **Divine Domains:** Knowledge
- **Casting Time:** 1 minute
- **Duration:** 1 watch
- **Range:** touch
- **Area:** 1 object
- **Save:** none
- **Components:** gestures

The caster touches a portal or singular object of no more than Medium size to set a magical alarm on it. For the spell's duration, the caster will be magically aware of any creature passing through the portal or touching the object.

*Upcasting*
The areas of multiple objects may be combined to affect a single larger object.

| Tier | Duration | Area      |
| :--: | :------- | :-------- |
|  2   | 1 day    | 2 objects |
|  3   | 1 week   | 3 objects |
|  +1  | +1 step  | +1 object |
## Alter Appearance
- **Arcane Traditions:** Illusion
- **Divine Domains:** Trickery
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Duration:** 1 turn (c)
- **Range:** personal
- **Area:** self
- **Save:** none
- **Components:** gestures & incantations

With this spell, a spellcaster can change their appearance to that of another humanoid creature of roughly the same size (within the same Size Category). The magical disguise may be seen through with a deliberate inspection and a [Perception](Skills.md#Perception) check versus the caster's spell save difficulty.

*Upcasting*
At higher levels, the caster may alter the appearance of others at longer ranges and for longer durations.

| Tier | Duration    | Range | Area         |
| :--: | :---------- | :---: | :----------- |
|  2   | 1 hour (c)  | touch | 1 creature   |
|  3   | 1 watch (c) |  30'  | 2 creatures  |
|  4   | 1 day (c)   |  60'  | 4 creatures  |
|  +1  | no change   | +30'  | +2 creatures |
## Arcane Arrow
- **Arcane Traditions:** Animism, Astromancy, Ausomancy, Conjuration, Necromancy, Sorcery, Thaumaturgy, Umbramancy
- **Divine Domains:** Air, Battle, Chaos, Cold, Death, Earth, Entropy, Fire, Light, Plants, Shadow, Water
- **Casting Time:** 1 action
- **Duration:** 10 minutes
- **Range:** 30'
- **Area:** 1 creature
- **Save:** none
- **Components:** gestures & incantations

The caster conjures an arrow of magical energy, whose damage type and appearance are determined by the tradition or domain used to cast the spell. Once cast, the spellcaster makes a Magical Attack roll vs the target's AC. On a successful hit, the spell does 1d6 damage of the given type. If the arrow is not loosed before the spell's duration expires, the arrow is lost.

| Domain/Tradition                   | Appearance        | Damage Type                                                                                                                                    |     |
| :--------------------------------- | :---------------- | :--------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- | --- |
| Air, Astromancy, Conjuration (air) | lightning         | lightning or thunder                                                                                                                           |     |
| Animism                            | 2' thorn or tooth | piercing                                                                                                                                       |     |
| Ausomancy, Light                   | glowing           | radiant                                                                                                                                        |     |
| Battle                             | an actual arrow   | piercing                                                                                                                                       |     |
| Chaos, Sorcery                     | violet glow       | d12: 1) acid, 2) blunt, 3) cold, 4) fire, 5) lightning, 6) necrotic, 7) piercing, 8) poison, 9) radiant, 10) slashing, 11) spirit, 12) thunder |     |
| Cold                               | ice               | cold or piercing                                                                                                                               |     |
| Conjuration (earth), Earth         | stone             | blunt, piercing or slashing                                                                                                                    |     |
| Conjuration (fire), Fire           | flame             | fire                                                                                                                                           |     |
| Conjuration (water), Water         | a bubbling liquid | acid or poison                                                                                                                                 |     |
| Death, Necromancy                  | blood             | necrotic                                                                                                                                       |     |
| Entropy                            | rotten and rusty  | necrotic                                                                                                                                       |     |
| Plant                              | 2' thorn          | piercing                                                                                                                                       |     |
| Shadow, Umbramancy                 | pure black        | cold or spirit                                                                                                                                 |     |
| Thaumaturgy                        | golden glow       | spirit                                                                                                                                         |     |

*Upcasting*
Additional arrows of the same type may be conjured at a rate of +1 arrow per +1 Tier. Each arrow may be targeted at a different creature. The arrows do not need to be loosed all at the same time, though any arrows not loosed by the end of the spell's duration are lost. Once all arrows are loosed, or the spell's duration expires, the spell ends.

| Tier | Duration   | Range | # Arrows |
| :--: | :--------- | :---: | :------: |
|  2   | 10 minutes |  60'  |    2     |
|  3   | 1 hour     |  90'  |    3     |
|  +1  | no change  | +30'  |    +1    |
